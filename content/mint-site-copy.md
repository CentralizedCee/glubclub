## GlubClub Token — site description

**GlubClub Token**
A utility and governance token for the GlubClub community. Use it to vote on
community decisions, and stake it to unlock higher membership tiers and
additional perks. The token is for participation and access — it carries no

## FAQ

**What is GlubClub?**
GlubClub is a community built around a Membership Pass and a
utility/governance token. The pass gets you into the community and its
perks; the token gives you a voice in how the community is run.

**What do I get with the Membership Pass?**
Community channel access, community roles, early access to drops and
events, and collectible status within GlubClub. It does not represent
ownership of the company or any entitlement to company reserves.

**What is the token for?**
It's a utility and governance token. Holding it lets you participate in
community votes; staking it unlocks additional membership tiers, perks, and
access. It is not equity and does not represent a claim on company assets.

**What does staking do?**
Staking your token unlocks higher membership tiers, perks, and access
within the community. It is not a mechanism for receiving payouts.

**Does GlubClub have company reserves / a treasury?**
The company holds reserves for its own operating purposes. These are
company reserves — not a fund held on behalf of pass or token holders.
Holding a Membership Pass or token does not entitle you to any portion of
these reserves or the company's financial performance.

**Are there token buybacks or burns?**
GlubClub may, from time to time and at its sole discretion, conduct token
buybacks or burns funded from general company revenue. These are not
scheduled in advance and are not tied to any formula, target, or
performance metric.
