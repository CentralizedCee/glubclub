# Wording rules (locked — do not revise without explicit human approval)

## Never use, anywhere
profit, returns, yield, invest / investment, guaranteed, backed by,
dividend, ROI, "your share of," "earn from our treasury," "passive income"

## One narrow carve-out — Terms/Legal Disclaimer page ONLY
Resolved 2026-09-05: a dedicated Terms of Service / Legal Disclaimer page
(NOT the FAQ, site copy, or any social/marketing content) may explicitly
state, in plain boilerplate legal language, that the Membership Pass and
Token are not investments or securities and carry no expectation of
profit — e.g. "The Membership Pass and Token are not investment contracts
or securities. There is no expectation of profit from the efforts of
GlubClub or any other party." This is standard, expected disclaimer
language, not promotional copy, and its absence protects nothing — the
avoidance-only approach used elsewhere is for promotional contexts
specifically, not a general ban on ever naming the concept.
This carve-out applies ONLY to a clearly-labeled legal/terms page. Every
other rule on this page still applies everywhere else, including this
same disclaimer's own surrounding boilerplate (don't drift into "so
naturally there's no yield" phrasing around it).

## Safe framing to use instead
- **NFT** → "membership pass," "access pass," "community pass"
- **Token** → "utility token," "governance token"
- **Treasury** → "company reserves," "treasury holdings" (never "your
  treasury" or "our shared treasury")
- **Buybacks/burns** → "periodic, at the company's discretion" — never tied
  to a stated formula, schedule, or performance metric
- **Staking** → "unlocks tiers/perks/access," never "earns" or "rewards you
  with value"

## When in doubt
Flag it for human review. Don't soften this list, don't add exceptions for
a specific piece of copy "just this once."
